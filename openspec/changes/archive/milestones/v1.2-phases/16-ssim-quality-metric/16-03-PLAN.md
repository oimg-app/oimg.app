# Metrics worker + singleton wrapper + FileEntry.metrics field + setFileMetric funnel + setFileResult invalidation

<objective>
Land the entire non-UI SSIM plumbing: a new sibling `metrics.worker.ts` that runs pure-JS SSIM on decoded raw+encoded ImageData (aligned via `@jsquash/resize`), a singleton `getMetricsWorker()` factory analogous to the codec `getPool()`, the `FileEntry.metrics` shape addition, the `setFileMetric` writer through the WR-02 `updateEntry` funnel, and — critically — the invalidation invariant on `setFileResult` that clears stale metrics on any re-encode.

Purpose: This plan puts every piece in place that 16-04 (the hook) needs to observe and dispatch, and it holds the cache-invalidation invariant that makes Phase 9 (`useLiveEncode`) re-encodes trigger automatic SSIM recompute "for free".

Output: Two new files (worker + singleton), edits to `FileEntry`, edits to two store functions, and extended `stores.test.ts` cases proving the funnel semantics.
</objective>
